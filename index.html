Computation · Intelligence · Materials

Agentic computing for materials & chemical discovery.

We connect quantum and atomistic simulations, statistical mechanics, curated data, and artificial intelligence to explore materials for energy and separations. Our next direction: scientific agents that plan calculations, use tools, and evaluate evidence.

Explore our research vision ↓

Research vision

Toward scientific superintelligence.

We aim to build scientific agents that turn questions into testable plans, connect reasoning with physical models, and learn from evidence—with researchers guiding the process.

Conceptual research workflow · Select a step

Define a useful question.

Start with a materials problem, such as a gas separation. Specify the target, operating conditions, and constraints before choosing a calculation.

Choose what to compute.

Select candidate materials, physical models, and calculations. Decide where more reasoning or new data would justify the computational cost.

Connect tools and data.

Use curated structures, molecular simulation, and predictive models to test the plan. Keep inputs, assumptions, and results traceable.

Decide what comes next.

Compare predictions with evidence and assess uncertainty. Refine the question, run another calculation, or stop when the evidence is sufficient.
