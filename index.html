AI-driven materials & chemical discovery.

We study nanoporous materials, especially metal–organic frameworks, and related material platforms for energy and separations. Our work combines quantum and atomistic simulations, statistical mechanics, curated data, and artificial intelligence to connect molecular behavior with material and process performance.

Research

We connect molecular-scale modeling, materials data, and process simulation to solve practical problems in energy and separations.

Applications

Carbon capture & gas separations · Hydrogen & methane storage · Electrochemical energy storage · Catalysis & conversion · Adsorption cooling

Materials

Reticular materials · Polymers · Carbons · Oxides · Electrolytes

01

Atomistic & Materials Modeling

Quantum chemistry, Monte Carlo, molecular dynamics, and enhanced sampling for structure, energetics, adsorption, diffusion, reaction, and electrochemical behavior.

02

Adsorption & Separation Processes

Molecular adsorption and transport models linked to pressure-, vacuum-, and temperature-swing performance and techno-economic evaluation.

03

Data-Driven Materials Discovery

Curated materials data, machine learning, and high-throughput screening for reproducible discovery and ranking.

04

AI-Enabled Research Automation

LLMs and tool-using agents that plan, execute, verify, and refine computational research workflows.
